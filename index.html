Meet the Founders & Artists

Dr. Akshay Shetti & Dr. Rachita Mustilwar • Kinusaiga Art

"Hello, we are Dr. Akshay Shetti (Founder) and Dr. Rachita Mustilwar (Co-Founder), the creators behind economic Kinusaiga Art and founders of Sri Raghavendra Anugraha Enterprises. What began as a fascination with traditional Japanese stitchless textile art has grown into a dedicated studio practice.

Every single artwork is born out of meticulous carving, fabric selection, and hours of patient craftsmanship. Our vision is simple: to make cost-effective, authentic, heirloom-quality handmade art accessible to every home and gifting occasion across India.

When you order from us, you aren't just buying wall décor—you are taking home a piece made with genuine dedication and love."

— Dr. Akshay Shetti & Dr. Rachita Mustilwar
Founder & Co-Founder, Kinusaiga Art
Sri Raghavendra Anugraha Enterprises
