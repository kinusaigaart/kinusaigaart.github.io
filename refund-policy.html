Refund & Return Policy

Transparency, authenticity, and care for our handmade creations

✦ Handcrafted Art Notice

Every piece created at Kinusaiga Art is an intricate, 100% handcrafted original made with hand-cut boards and individually tucked Japanese fabrics. Because each artwork requires substantial time, effort, and personalized craftsmanship, we follow a strict no-refund and no-return policy on all completed sales.

1. General Sales Policy

Once an order is confirmed, packed, or dispatched, cancellations, standard returns, and refunds are not permitted. We encourage all buyers to carefully review the photos, dimensions, and product details on our website or over WhatsApp before making payment.

2. Damaged in Transit (Exceptions)

We inspect every artwork thoroughly and use multi-layer shockproof bubble wrap with reinforced outer packaging. However, if an artwork arrives damaged due to postal or courier handling:

  • A replacement with another available item or a refund will only be considered if severe in-transit damage is conclusively proven.
  • Replacement is strictly subject to availability, as each handmade art piece is unique and cannot be mass-reproduced instantly.
  • If an identical piece is unavailable, you will have the option to pick an alternative artwork of equal value or receive a refund.

3. Mandatory Unpacking / Unboxing Video Requirement

⚠️ Crucial Requirement for Damage Claims:

A clear, continuous, uncut 360° unpacking video from start to finish is mandatory to claim any transit damage. Without an unboxing video, damage claims cannot be entertained under any circumstances.

Your unboxing video must follow these conditions:

  • The video must clearly show the package in its original, sealed state before any tape is cut.
  • The shipping label with the tracking details and recipient address must be clearly visible.
  • The video must be recorded in one single continuous take without cuts, edits, or pauses.
  • The damage to the frame or fabric artwork must be clearly pointed out as the package is opened.

4. Claim Reporting Window

Any transit damage claim along with the uncut unboxing video must be shared with us within 24 hours of parcel delivery. Claims reported after 24 hours of delivery status cannot be accepted.

Have an Issue With Your Delivery?

If your package arrived damaged, please share your uncut unboxing video and order details directly with our studio on WhatsApp within 24 hours.
